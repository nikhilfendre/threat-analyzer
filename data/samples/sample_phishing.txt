THREAT ADVISORY TA-2026-0091 — Credential Phishing Campaign

Between April 2 and April 5, 2026, a spearphishing campaign targeted
employees of multiple logistics companies. The attackers sent spoofed
emails impersonating the IT helpdesk, warning recipients that their
password would expire within 24 hours.

Each email contained a link to http://login-portal-verify[.]tk/reset,
a credential harvesting page mimicking the corporate single sign-on.
Victims who entered their usernames and passwords had their credentials
stolen immediately. In at least twelve cases the attackers then used the
compromised credentials to log in to the VPN and conduct internal
reconnaissance.

The phishing kit also attempted to harvest multi-factor authentication
tokens by proxying the real login page in real time. The campaign
infrastructure reused the domain mail-auth-service[.]xyz, previously
seen in a 2025 phishing wave, hosted at 91.203.67.114.

The attached HTML file invoice-april.html (md5:
d41d8cd98f00b204e9800998ecf8427e) contained JavaScript that redirected
users to the harvesting page when opened in a browser.

No malware was deployed and no data encryption occurred. The primary
impact is credential theft affecting roughly 60 user accounts. The SOC
recommends forcing password resets and enforcing MFA for all remote
access.

Indicators observed: the sender address support@helpdesk-update[.]com,
the harvesting URL above, and the HTML attachment hash.
